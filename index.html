About

Hi, I'm Mae

Graphic and web designer with ten years of experience, six of them in real estate, who also enjoys handling the tasks that keep an agent's marketing running.

Most of my real estate work has been on the creative side: social posts, flyers, videos and email campaigns. Along the way, I've also helped with the support work around it, like scheduling content, managing emails, assisting with admin tasks and keeping files organized.

I'm organized, dependable and easy to work with. I follow instructions closely, keep you updated, and treat your business like it matters, because it does.

Outside of work, I enjoy the outdoors, music, and reading.
