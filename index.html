About

Hi, I'm Mae

Graphic and web designer with over ten years of experience, now specializing in real estate graphic design — delivering minimalist, visually striking work.

My design philosophy centers on simplicity and clean aesthetics: less is more, and effective communication comes through thoughtful design. I take a fresh approach to each project, making sure my clients' vision is understood and delivered.

Outside of designing, I enjoy the outdoors, music, and reading — a good hike or a quiet walk is usually where the next idea shows up.
